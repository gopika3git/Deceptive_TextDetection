      Phase 10: Privacy, Security & Data Governance

      Historical writing samples constitute sensitive personal information. VeritasAI enforces strict safeguards:

      • Local Storage Isolation: All personal profiles and historical writing samples are stored locally in isolated files (profiles_store/) on your machine.
      • Explicit Authorization: New profiles require explicit confirmation that the user is authorized to analyze the author's writing.
      • Zero External Transmission: Private text is never sent to third-party APIs or external cloud servers.
      • No Model Retraining Without Consent: Personal historical text is never silently fed into training sets.
      • Permanent Data Purge: Deleting a sample or profile permanently removes the text from disk.
      • Sanitized Logging: Application logs do not record complete private messages.
